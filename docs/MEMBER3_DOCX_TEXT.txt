2. Project Context & End-to-End Intelligence Pipeline
Fixed continuous ambient air quality monitoring stations (CAAQMS) provide accurate point measurements, but leave spatial blind spots between stations across Indian metropolitan areas. AeroSentinel combines multi-source environmental sensing with spatial modeling to detect potential emerging pollution events in these unmonitored zones.
The data flows through the following pipeline:

Tested Model Candidates & Progressive Hierarchy
Three model architectures were trained and benchmarked:
[Baseline 1: Logistic Regression]
         ↓ (PR-AUC: 0.468, F1: 0.441)
[Tree Model 2: Balanced Random Forest]
         ↓ (PR-AUC: 0.684, F1: 0.613)
[Production Model 3: Platt Sigmoid-Calibrated Random Forest (cv=3)]
         → (PR-AUC: 0.742, F1: 0.679, ROC-AUC: 0.891, Brier: 0.0482)
Baseline 1: Logistic Regression (L2 Regularized)
Library:
 scikit-learn (sklearn.linear_model.LogisticRegression).
Hyperparameters:
 C=1.0, penalty='l2', class_weight='balanced', max_iter=500, random_state=42.
Tree Model 2: Balanced Random Forest Classifier
Library:
 scikit-learn (sklearn.ensemble.RandomForestClassifier).
Hyperparameters:
 n_estimators=100, max_depth=12, min_samples_split=5, class_weight='balanced', random_state=42, n_jobs=-1.
Production Model 3: Platt Sigmoid-Calibrated Random Forest
Library:
 scikit-learn (sklearn.calibration.CalibratedClassifierCV).
Architecture:
 3-fold internal cross-validation fitting a sigmoid logistic link function directly over the out-of-fold probability estimates of the Balanced Random Forest base estimator (method='sigmoid', cv=3).
Operational Threshold:
 Set to 
0.20
 calibrated probability to maximize recall during critical public health episodes.
6. Hotspot Target Generation & Imbalance Audit
Because official municipal regulatory event citations do not exist in historical open data, inventing synthetic event boundaries would introduce severe confirmation bias. We used the statutory CPCB limit (
60.0 μ
g/m
3
) as a proxy label.
An audit of the chronological partitions revealed a distinct shift in environmental regimes: 
Training Partition:
 6,526 positives out of 49,120 rows (
13.29% positive rate
). Represents mixed seasonal baseline conditions. 
Validation Partition:
 39 positives out of 10,528 rows (
0.37% positive rate
). Represents a clean monsoon window with high meteorological precipitation and heavy washout. 
Test Partition:
 1,246 positives out of 10,528 rows (
11.84% positive rate
). Represents an elevated winter/post-monsoon inversion period characterized by shallow planetary boundary layer heights and surface smoke accumulation. 
Imbalance Strategy:
 Synthetic minority oversampling (SMOTE) was rejected because synthesizing points across continuous space-time coordinates breaks geodesic and temporal autocorrelations. Class imbalance was handled using 
cost-sensitive learning
 via class_weight='balanced' inside the tree split criteria, paired with post-hoc 
Platt sigmoid calibration
 to convert skewed tree voting margins into calibrated posterior probabilities.

13. Evidence Generation Layer & Non-Causal Attribution
Implemented in ai-service/ml/explainability/evidence.py, this layer translates model inputs and sensor readings into traceable, structured evidence payloads without making unsupported causal claims.
Governance Rules for Generated Text
Approved Wording:
 "Associated contributing signal", "Correlated remote-sensing anomaly", "Proximate spatial indicator".
Prohibited Wording:
 "Factory X caused the pollution", "Satellite proves violation", "Sensor failure confirms clean air".
Evidence Classification Structure
Evidence items are grouped into four categories:
DIRECT_OBSERVATION: Continuous CAAQMS ground sensor measurements (
PM
2.5
, 
PM
10
) compared to regional spatial baselines.
REMOTE_SENSING: NASA FIRMS active fire plume vectors aligned with prevailing winds, and Sentinel-5P tropospheric column levels.
GIS_CONTEXT: Proximity to municipal MIDC industrial polygons or major traffic corridors.
MONITORING_COVERAGE: Distance to nearest ground station and network blind-spot indicators.

19. Core Engineering Decisions
Platt Sigmoid Calibration over Isotonic Regression:
Decision:
 Adopted CalibratedClassifierCV(method='sigmoid', cv=3).
Reason:
 Isotonic regression is prone to overfitting and step-function distortion when minority class samples are constrained. Platt sigmoid scaling provides a smooth, monotonic probability calibration curve.
Separate Regressors per Horizon vs. Direct Multi-Output Tree:
Decision:
 Trained three independent Random Forest regressors (
1
h
, 
3
h
, 
6
h
).
Reason:
 Each forecast horizon is driven by different physical dynamics (microscale auto-regressive momentum dominates 
T+1
h
, while regional meteorological advection dominates 
T+6
h
). Independent models allow tree splits to specialize by horizon.
Empirical Residual Quantiles over Gaussian Assumptions:
Decision:
 Extracted non-parametric validation residuals (
P
10
,
P
90
) to construct forecast bounds.
Reason:
 Environmental sensor error distributions exhibit skewness and heavy tails, which violate parametric Gaussian assumptions (
y±1.96σ
).
20. Known Limitations
Geographic Scope:
 Models are currently fitted to the topography and emissions profile of the Pune Metropolitan Region (PMR). Deploying to new urban areas requires retraining on local meteorological dynamics and spatial network topologies.
Daytime Satellite Coverage Gaps:
 Sentinel-5P overpasses occur in the early afternoon, resulting in zero optical satellite updates during nocturnal hours. The pipeline relies on active fire lookbacks, ground spatial lags, and meteorology during these intervals.
